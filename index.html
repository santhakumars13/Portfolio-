01 // METHODOLOGY

How I Approach Data Science

⚙

Data Preparation Over Model Complexity

Most real-world models fail because of leakage, collinearity, and unhandled outliers—not because the algorithm wasn't advanced enough. I spend 70% of my cycle analyzing distributions, imputing safely, and building reproducible preprocessing pipelines.

Handling Skewness Feature Scaling Target Encoding
📈

Metrics That Match Business Cost

Accuracy is misleading on skewed data. I evaluate with Precision-Recall curves, Cost-Matrix thresholds, and Confusion Matrices.

🖈

Actionable Reporting

Insights that stay in a Jupyter Notebook don't deliver value. I build self-serve Power BI and Tableau interfaces for stakeholders to interrogate the data directly.
